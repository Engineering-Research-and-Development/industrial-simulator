External data ingestion

        
Assets in external or hybrid synchronisation mode (Inspector → Industrial profile) are driven by
          envelopes received over REST (POST /api/ingestion/events) or MQTT. Envelope:
          {schema_version, event_id, asset_id, event_type, timestamp, measurements | state | cycle | product | config}.
